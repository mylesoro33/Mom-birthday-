    the long version

    A Note

    Dear Mom,

    Happy birthday. I wanted to make you something you could keep and come back to, so I built you this.

    I don't say it enough, but thank you for working so hard for us. You are one of the hardest-working people I know, and I've watched you do it my whole life without ever asking for anything back. So much of what you do, you do for your kids, and I see that more clearly every year.

    Thank you for always helping me. Every time I've called, every time I've needed something, you've been there, usually before I even finished asking. You care about my hobbies like they're your own, and you've never once made me feel like I was too much.

    I hope today you get to slow down and let us take care of you for a change. I hope the new car makes every drive a little more fun. And tonight at dinner, I hope you know how proud I am to be your son.

    Love,Myles
